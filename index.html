How to test

  1. Print the mat at 100% — not “fit to page.”
  2. Volume up, silent switch off. Every scan opens with two seconds of soft chirps — the sound checks. Hold the phone over the empty table where the object will sit and keep still until they finish.
  3. Then put the object in the middle of the mat.
  4. Tap Start and walk slowly around it twice — first pass level with it, second pass a little higher. The app takes the photos by itself and chirps softly for each photo it keeps; just keep the object filling at least half the screen.
  5. Use good light. Keep the object still. Quiet room — no TV or talking while you scan.
